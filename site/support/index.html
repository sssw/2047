A little help.

Swipe on the board or use arrow keys, WASD, or the direction buttons. Open How to play for merge rules. Settings lets you hide controls, change the theme, or copy your saved-game link.

Try a timed round

Tap the timer beside 2047 to set a countdown. The countdown is off by default, with a 2-minute preset. Your first valid move starts the clock. Without a time limit, the same display shows elapsed time. When time runs out, moves stop and your maximum tile and score appear beside the game. Once started, the clock keeps running in menus and other tabs. Reloading and saved-game links preserve its starting time.

Tap the mode name to open new-game settings. Starting a new round with a nonzero score asks for confirmation.

Continue a game

Progress saves automatically in the current browser. Private browsing, blocked storage, or clearing site data may prevent recovery. If the page says “Session only”, copy a saved-game link before closing it. Each domain and device keeps separate saves.

Share a challenge

Use Share in the game or Copy saved-game link in Settings. In a seeded game, choose “Share the starting seed instead” to start from the same board as a friend. Random-game links keep the shared position, with fresh random tiles afterward.
