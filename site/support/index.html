A little help.

Swipe on the board or use arrow keys, WASD, or the direction buttons. Open How to play for merge rules. Settings lets you hide controls, change the theme, or copy your saved-game link.

Continue a game

Progress saves automatically in the current browser. Private browsing, blocked storage, or clearing site data may prevent recovery. If the page says “Session only”, copy a saved-game link before closing it. Each domain and device keeps separate saves.

Share a challenge

Use Share in the game or Copy saved-game link in Settings. Choose “Share the starting seed instead” to start from the same board as a friend.
