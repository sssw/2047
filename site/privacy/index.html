Your game stays here.

2047 requires no account and includes no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts.

Your progress, recent sessions, best scores, and preferences are stored in this browser’s local storage. They are not uploaded by the game. Clear this website’s browser data to remove them. Use ?save=0 to play without saving.

Sharing a game creates a link containing its board, score, and generation mode, plus the seed and deterministic random state for seeded games. Anyone with that link can read and play the shared position. Do not put personal information in a seed. Snapshot data is placed in the URL fragment, which is not sent in ordinary HTTP requests. Query parameters, including seed challenges, are sent to the hosting service.

The hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as IP address and browser details, to deliver and secure the website. External links, including the App Store and support site, follow their own privacy policies.

Local saves are separate on each domain and device. Use a saved-game link to transfer progress.
