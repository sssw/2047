Every bit counts.

Slide the board. Compatible neighbors merge once per move. Every valid move adds one new tile.

1 01+2 10=3 11

Each tile’s small squares show its binary value, read left to right, top to bottom. Outlined squares are 1; gray squares are 0. Leading empty spaces are not bits.

No overlapping bits? Add the numbers. For example, 1 + 2 = 3 and 2 + 5 = 7.

Identical, all-one bits? They merge too: 1 + 1 = 2, 3 + 3 = 6, and 7 + 7 = 14. Other equal numbers, like 2 + 2, cannot merge in Classic mode.

Reach 2047, then keep playing. Your score adds the value of every merged tile.

Other modes & shortcuts

Insane: identical all-one tiles advance to the next power of two: 3 + 3 = 4, 7 + 7 = 8.

2048: equal tiles merge; each new tile is 2. Reach 2048.

Arrows or WASD to move. Z to undo, Y to redo, N for a new game, ? for help. Swipe on the board on touch screens.
